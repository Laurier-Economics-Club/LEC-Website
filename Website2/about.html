Field notes

The Laurier Economics Club helps students explore economics through academic support, professional events, original research, mentorship, and community.

Whether you’re pursuing finance, policy, consulting, or academia, LEC gives you the tools, network, and experience to get there. We bridge the gap between classroom theory and real-world application.

Our History

18 years of building something meaningful

From a small group of economics students to one of Laurier’s most active clubs.

  1. 2008

    LEC is Founded

    Established at Wilfrid Laurier University as a dedicated space for economics students to connect, learn, and grow beyond the lecture hall.

  2. 2010s

    Club Expands its Programs

    LEC formalizes four pillars — Academic Success, Social Connection, Personal Growth, and Professional Development — and builds structured events, workshops, and peer mentorship.

  3. 2015

    Research Publications Launch

    First student-authored economics newsletter and research reports give members a platform for original analysis and policy writing.

  4. 2018

    Mentorship Program Introduced

    Upper-year students are paired with first- and second-year members for academic and career guidance.

  5. 2020

    LEC Goes Virtual

    Programming moves online through the pandemic while events, workshops, and community continue.

  6. 2022

    Corporate Relations Expand

    Firm visits and sponsor relationships create pipelines into finance, consulting, and economics employers.

  7. 2024

    Multi-University Case Competition

    LEC’s flagship competition brings Ontario universities together around real-world economic challenges.

  8. 2025

    New Brand Identity

    A refined crest logo, updated green and gold palette, and modernized digital presence mark the current era.

  9. 2025–26

    28 Executives, Growing Strong

    Under Co-Presidents Kavay Kapoor and Aaryan Bhavsar, the executive continues academics, research, events, mentorship, and partnerships for Laurier’s economics community.
