Field notes

Economics isn’t just something you study. It’s something you live.

The Laurier Economics Club connects Wilfrid Laurier students to ideas, academic support, and professional rooms they might not walk into alone. Mock exams, mentorship, trivia, case work, debates, and Industry Night sit alongside the quieter work of finding people who also want to know how the world is priced.

You do not need a finished theory of everything to belong here. Curiosity is the entry requirement.

Index

Six ways into the work

Not a menu of identical offerings. A working index of how LEC actually spends its time.

  1. 01

    Mock Exams

    Ticketed sessions for EC120, EC140, and EC295. Exam-style questions, in person or online, before the real thing.

    See events
  2. 02

    Econ Trivia

    Teams of two to four. Sports in Economics nights put students across the table from professors.

  3. 03

    Mentorship

    First-year Business and Economics students paired with upper years on classes, co-op, and the map of the degree.

    Join pathway
  4. 04

    Case Competitions

    Applied economics under time pressure, including joint cases with the UW Economics Society.

  5. 05

    Industry Night

    Business-formal networking with practitioners. Past programmes have included a real-estate focus, with RBC and EQ Bank as event partners.

  6. 06

    Debates & Workshops

    The Great Economics Debate, graduate-school workshops, and sessions that treat argument as a skill.

Classroom model

Set a price. Watch the quantities move.

An illustrative supply-and-demand market. Nothing here is real data. Move the hypothetical price and see quantity demanded, quantity supplied, and whether the market is short, long, or at rest.

Equilibrium in this sketch is where the two schedules cross: the price at which buyers want exactly as much as sellers will offer.
